# Poster Session

A poster session will be held for all EXPRA groups, providing an opportunity for the student teams to present their research projects and discuss their results with fellow students and instructors. During the session, each group will present its work in the form of a scientific poster and will be available to answer questions and engage in discussions.

**When?** 9 February 2026, 14:00–18:00  
**Where?** Seminarpavillon, rooms SP 2.03 & 2.04

<embed src="../_static/lageplan.pdf" width="100%" height="600px" type="application/pdf">

The poster session is designed as an open-format event, allowing visitors to move freely between posters and interact with the presenting groups. Detailed information on poster format and layout will be provided by your course instructors in advance.

The printing of the posters as well as bringing them to the session will be organized and fully covered by the organizers. Please ensure that your poster is sent to your course instructors no later than 2 February.
